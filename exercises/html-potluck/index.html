Cheesecake

INGREDIENTS

  • 32 ounces of full-fat brick cream cheese
  • 1 cup of granulated sugar
  • 3 large eggs
  • 1 teaspoon of vanilla extract
  • 1.5 cups of graham cracker crumbs
  • 4 tablespoons of melted butter
  • 2 tablespoons of sugar

INSTRUCTIONS

  1. Set your oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. Mix the graham cracker crumbs, sugar, and melted butter until it feels like wet sand.
  3. Press the mixture firmly into the bottom and slightly up the sides of a 9-inch springform pan.
  4. Bake for 10 to 15 minutes, then let it cool.




Brownies

INGREDIENTS

  • 1/2 cup (113g) unsalted butter, melted
  • 1 cup (200g) granulated white sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 teaspoon pure vanilla extract
  • 1/2 cup (34g) unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 3/4 cup (90g) all-purpose flour
  • 1/4 to 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 cup chocolate chips or chopped chocolate (optional)

INSTRUCTIONS

  1. Heat your oven to 350°F (175°C) and grease an 8x8 inch baking pan.
  2. Whisk the melted butter (or oil) and sugar together in a medium bowl. Add the eggs and vanilla extract, then mix well.
  3. Sift together the flour, cocoa powder, salt, and baking powder in a separate small bowl.
  4. Gently stir the dry mixture into the wet ingredients until just combined. Fold in the chocolate chips.
  5. Pour the batter into your prepared pan. Bake for 20 to 22 minutes.
  6. Let the brownies cool completely in the pan before cutting them into squares.




Red Velvet Cookies

INGREDIENTS

  • 1 2/3 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/4 cup unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 1 teaspoon of Baking Soda
  • 1/4 teaspoon of Salt
  • 1/2 cup unsalted butter, softened
  • 3/4 cup brown sugar and 1/4 cup white sugar
  • 1 large egg and 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  • 3/4 teaspoon red gel food coloring
  • 1 cup white chocolate chips

INSTRUCTIONS

  1. Whisk flour, cocoa powder, baking soda, and salt in a bowl.
  2. Beat softened butter and both sugars until fluffy.
  3. Mix in the egg, vanilla, and red gel food coloring.
  4. Slowly blend the dry mix into the wet mix just until combined.
  5. Gently stir in the white chocolate chips.
  6. Refrigerate the dough for 30 minutes.
  7. Scoop balls onto a lined sheet and bake at 350°F (175°C) for 10 to 12 minutes.
